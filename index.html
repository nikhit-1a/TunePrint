About Tuneprint

Tuneprint is a web application that allows you to find songs by simply humming or singing the lyrics. Our hybrid audio processing engine automatically transcribes your singing and matches it against a massive music database in real-time.

Powered by: ACRCloud for acoustic fingerprinting, Deepgram for AI speech-to-text, and Apple iTunes Search for the metadata library.

Disclaimer: This web application is strictly not for commercial use. It is provided for educational and non-profit purposes.
